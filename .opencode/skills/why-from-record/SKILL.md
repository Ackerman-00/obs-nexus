---
name: why-from-record
description: Answer why from the repo record before planning a fix: cite commit, AGENTS.md section, issue or relay run; mark inferences.
---

Before a non-trivial change, grep git log, AGENTS.md, issues and the relay for
the decision. Quote the commit or section that made the rule. If the record is
silent, say `inference:` explicitly and name what would confirm it. Do not
invent a rationale that sounds plausible.
