---
name: repo-improvement
description: Autonomous improvement each run: probed brainstorm, landed change, evidence-backed; do not repeat the same small patch.
---

Every run must produce a `why:` line with a cited source and an `improve:`
line with >=5 candidates and `landed: <sha or issue #>`. Probe at least one
assumption before choosing. Prefer a structural fix that removes repeated
maintenance over another one-line patch. Record the probe output in the relay.
